Evaluation Criteria

How your live project is scored on final day.

01

Innovation & Novelty

Uniqueness of the solution, intellectual depth of the AI/tech model, and divergence from existing common market alternatives.

02

Technical Execution

Robustness of the working demo, code architecture, integration of required data models, and stability under testing scenarios.

03

Business Viability

Market scalability, identification of active funding pathways, and validity of the business model presentation over pure code.

PROBLEM 01Healthcare AI & MedTech Platform

AI-Powered Rural Diagnostic & Patient Triage Platform

Diagnostic AI screeners and clinic SaaS networks for hospitals, clinics and telehealth providers.

Description
  • Problem: Rural areas lack access to specialist doctors.
  • Example: AI skin disease detection smartphone app.
  • Example: Automated X-ray bone fracture detection tool.
  • Example: Smart hospital queue and patient triage system.
  • Covered Artifacts: ML Guide & Hospital Management Apps.
  • Focus: Diagnostic AI screeners and clinic SaaS networks.
Deliverables
  • Working medical image classifier or triage demo.
  • Diagnostic safety and non-clinical disclaimer plan.
  • System architecture and diagnostic pipeline diagram.
  • HIPAA and patient data privacy plan.
  • Target customer definition (hospitals, clinics, telehealth).
  • Pitch covering healthcare SaaS model and market.
