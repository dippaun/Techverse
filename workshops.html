M

Program Mission

Why this workshop exists

Empower student teams to transform innovative ideas into fully functional, competition-ready prototypes across all six arenas through structured mentoring, engineering excellence, rapid prototyping, rigorous testing, and impactful, pitch-ready presentations.
R

Day-by-Day Roadmap

Ten days, five phases, one competition-ready prototype

Phase 1 · Ideation & Planning
Day01

Discover · Evaluate · Inspire

Assess team skills, review ideas and prior projects, identify innovation potential, form teams, and finalize the most promising concepts.

Day02

Strategize · Plan · Resource

Freeze project scope, prepare the Bill of Materials, allocate team responsibilities, and identify required hardware and software resources.

Phase 2 · Design & Build
Day03

Learn · Design · Architect

Understand system architecture, core hardware components, sensors, coding workflows, simulations, and curated learning resources.

Day04

Build · Assemble · Configure

Begin hardware assembly, electronics integration, core system setup, and the initial coding pass.

Day05

Develop · Integrate · Validate

Integrate sensors and autonomous algorithms, and begin subsystem-level testing and validation.

Phase 3 · Review & Optimize
Day06

Review · Debug · Present

Resolve outstanding issues, track progress, and begin building the presentation, documentation, and project storytelling.

Day07

Optimize & Complete

Finish system integration, improve reliability, and carry out complete functionality testing.

Phase 4 · Test & Rehearse
Day08

Benchmark & Assess

Evaluate readiness, identify gaps, optimize performance, and complete any pending modules.

Day09

Test · Refine · Rehearse

Conduct trial runs and debugging, practice mock judging sessions, and align delivery with evaluation criteria.

Phase 5 · Launch
Day10

Demonstrate · Impress · Win

Deliver the final demonstration, explain technical innovations with clarity, and answer judges confidently and persuasively.

S

Success Indicators

What a competition-ready team looks like at the end

✓Innovative and practical solution
✓Reliable, working functionality
✓Strong technical implementation
✓Professional documentation & presentation
✓Team collaboration and confidence
✓High alignment with Techfest judging criteria
✓ Guided, pitch-ready presentation session — every team rehearses and refines their final pitch with mentor feedback before demo day
Mentor's Vision

Goal: Every team should leave the workshop with a competition-ready prototype, a compelling technical story, and the confidence to compete at national-level innovation events.

Final Point · Pitch-Ready Guidance

Beyond building the prototype, mentors dedicate a focused pitch-readiness session in the closing days — coaching every team on narrative structure, live-demo delivery, and confident Q&A handling, so they walk into judging as polished and prepared as their prototype.
